1. Project Title Justification –


Anti-Spects Sleeping The title Anti-Spects Sleeping is derived from its core purpose—preventing damage to spectacles during unintentional sleep. It creatively combines “Anti” (preventive) and “Spects” (spectacles), indicating a smart system designed to detect sleep posture and protect eyewear from accidental breakage. This name reflects the project's intent in a concise and impactful way.

2. Technical Overview


This system involves posture detection using basic sensors, potentially including tilt sensors or IR modules. When it senses the user is lying down or showing drowsy behavior, it triggers a gentle alert—either a buzzer or vibration motor—reminding them to remove their spectacles. The simplicity of the circuit design, combined with real-world utility, makes it an ideal project for practical learning in electronics and embedded systems.

3. Relevance to EEE Domain


The project makes use of key EEE concepts such as sensor interfacing, signal conditioning, and basic actuator control. It demonstrates how electronics can solve everyday human problems with user-friendly applications. It also encourages innovative thinking in wearable technology and human-centric design—important themes in modern electronics and embedded systems engineering.
